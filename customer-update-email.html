We've been working hard to improve your RankdSEO experience. Here's what's new and how it benefits you:

We've significantly improved page loading speed with smart pagination. The opportunities list now loads instantly, making your backlink research workflow much faster.

Check out the new Activity Log in your dashboard to see platform updates, new features, and important announcements - all in one place.

Impact: You'll always know what's new without needing to check emails.

🔧 Report Broken Opportunities

Found a broken link or outdated opportunity? You can now report issues directly from any opportunity page. Our team will review and fix them quickly.

Impact: Help us keep the database high-quality for everyone.

We've optimized the entire platform for search engines with improved metadata, structured data, and a dynamic sitemap. This helps more people discover quality backlink opportunities.

Impact: Behind-the-scenes improvement - no action needed from you.

  • All your saved projects and campaigns are safe
  • Your account settings and preferences are unchanged
  • Access to all 1,000+ backlink opportunities
